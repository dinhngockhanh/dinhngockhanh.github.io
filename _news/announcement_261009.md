We and our collaborators at IICD introduced [Songbird](https://academic.oup.com/nar/article/54/18/gkag960/8892726), a new method to infer single-cell copy number profiles.
[CINner](https://github.com/dinhngockhanh/CINner) was used to ascertain Songbird's performance.
Read more about Songbird in the [IICD press release](https://cancerdynamics.columbia.edu/news/songbird-takes-flight-single-cell-genomics).
